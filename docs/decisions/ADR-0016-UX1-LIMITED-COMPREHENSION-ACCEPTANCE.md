# ADR-0016 — MP-UX1 limited comprehension acceptance (owner ruling)

- **Status:** **Accepted — owner ruling 2026-10-31.** The Product/UX owner was asked how MP-UX1's comprehension exit should be judged after P03 answered all eight prompts as understood while independent timing, free-text interpretation and action traces were absent, and the external review held that self-report alone cannot be MET. The owner selected an **explicit limited acceptance** of the P03 self-report, recorded verbatim in [participant-p03.json](../development/evidence-published/ux1-prototype-latest/participant-p03.json) and the plan §19.103.
- **Decision:** MP-UX1's comprehension exit is **`MET_LIMITED` by explicit owner acceptance**. It is a bounded acceptance of the recorded participant self-report, not an objective verification of comprehension, not a measurement, and not product acceptance.
- **What it accepts:** P03's eight selected comprehension answers on the approved build (product source `4dd011a`, executable `sha256:792212d9…`, fixture `sha256:2e33ed06…`) covering the approved Flow diagram with its lists, the Compare next step and pair completion, the Viewed-versus-Runtime-owned-formal-selection wording, and the disconnected intervention action.
- **What it does not accept:** independent five-second timing; free-text interpretation of the answers; objective operation traces; representative sampling; real Runtime graphs or media; DPI/High-Contrast/screen-reader behaviour; performance or record acceptance of any other task. Those stay `NOT_MEASURED`.
- **Conditions on the acceptance:** (1) no document may cite this ruling as measured evidence or as a product/UX acceptance of the shipping UI; (2) a future participant session with independent timing and operation instrumentation is still preferred and would upgrade, not repeat, this record; (3) product promotion, Compare implementation (MP-CMP1), bounded-preview media (MP-IMG5), production Flow stages (MP-FLOW2/3) and release keep their own contracts and authorities; (4) the P01 and P02 records stay unchanged.
- **Why a limited ruling is legitimate:** [DC-MP-UX1](../architecture/DESKTOP_DESIGN_CONTRACTS.md#dc-mp-ux1) requires a validation record with the eight checks, per-participant observations and explicit failures; it does not make the prototype's self-report equivalent to measurement. ADR-0008 §5 permits owner-adopted documented values; this ruling adopts a documented record with its stated limits rather than inventing behaviour.
- **Evidence:** [participant-p01.json](../development/evidence-published/ux1-prototype-latest/participant-p01.json) 0/3/5, [participant-p02.json](../development/evidence-published/ux1-prototype-latest/participant-p02.json) 3/5/0, [participant-p03.json](../development/evidence-published/ux1-prototype-latest/participant-p03.json) 8/0/0, [repair-smoke.json](../development/evidence-published/ux1-prototype-latest/repair-smoke.json) review and Gate history. MP-TYP1 125 % capture HOLD and MP-R0B HOLD are untouched by this ruling.
